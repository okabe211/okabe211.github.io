Privacy Policy

WebView (“we”, “us”) made Desi Prank Sounds (the “app”), a free app with ads. This policy explains what the app does with information. Effective October 30, 2026.

The short version

We have no accounts, no servers and no analytics of our own. Your favorites, recently played sounds, custom prank modes, play statistics and settings stay on your phone. The only information that leaves your phone is what Google’s advertising code collects to show ads, described below.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your favorites, recently played sounds, custom prank modes, combo and badge progress, language and switch settings on your device. We never receive this data. Clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the app deletes it. If Android backup is on for your phone, Android may also back this data up to your own Google account.

Permissions the app uses

Vibration: optional vibration with sounds. Camera light: only if you switch on Flash sync; the app uses the flashlight and does not take photos or video. The app does not use your location, contacts, microphone, photos or files.

Sharing a sound

If you tap Share, the app makes a temporary copy of that sound file and hands it to the app you pick (for example a messenger). We do not see what you share or with whom.

Ads and Google

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use your advertising ID, IP address (and the approximate location derived from it), device and app information such as model, operating system and app version, and how you interact with ads. Depending on your region and choices, ads can be personalized. See Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. The app uses Google Play services and Google’s User Messaging Platform for consent.

We do not sell your information and we do not combine ad data with anything stored in the app.

Your choices

In the European Economic Area and the UK (and where else the law requires), the app asks for your consent before requesting ads. You can change it any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. On Android you can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your phone’s Settings → Privacy → Ads. Residents of some US states have the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal data; use the same Ad privacy choices option or Android’s ads settings.

Children

The app is meant for people 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 and set ads to a teen content rating. If you believe a child has given information to Google through the app, contact us at toptenbestindia@gmail.com and we will help.

Security and retention

We keep nothing on servers, so there is nothing for us to secure or delete beyond the data on your device. Data held by Google is governed by Google’s policies and controls.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be in the app and at the same web address, with a new effective date.
